# Thesis

**Elevation should read like race-car telemetry for your body: one volt signal on matte black, every number big enough to read mid-set, and every reward earned by a real PR.**

# MASTER anti-slop list
1. No purple, indigo or violet anywhere; no multi-stop gradients.
2. No slate (#0F172A / #1E293B) rounded-2xl card grids.
3. No glassmorphism, backdrop blur or neumorphism.
4. No emoji as UI icons; one icon set with a consistent stroke.
5. No 4-equal-KPI-tile hero; one dominant number per screen.
6. No body font for numbers; tabular mono only.
7. No accent overuse: volt covers 10% of pixels or less and is kept for action, state and reward.
8. No looping or pulsing ambient animation; no UI motion over 300ms.
9. No confetti; the PR reward is the scanline plus the chip, nothing else.
10. No touch target under 48px; no primary action in the top half of the phone screen.
11. No center-aligned paragraphs; no generic copy ("Welcome back!", "Unlock your potential").
12. No ruled-paper or grid background textures.
13. No fake data in real views; demo mode is clearly bannered.
14. No device-mockup share card; the share card is its own composition.
15. No contrast below WCAG 2.2 AA; focus rings are never removed.
